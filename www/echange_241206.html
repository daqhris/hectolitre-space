FOUNTAIN HEAD
by Ana(na)t (maria) Mart.kovich (AKA Mrs. So Bitch)

Open studio

To mark our stay (and it's ending) at the Hectolitre artist residency, we would like to invite you for an 'open studio', and share a new body of work. Fountain Head, consisting of text, videos and installations made for and with Hectolitre, Jeu De Balle and Brussels.

-
Fiday-Saturday, 06th and 7th of November, 2024
@Reactor, Rue de l'Hectolitre 3, Brussels

From 11:00h until 18:00h, Friday and Saturday
-
For those of you who wish to get to know us better:

https://thank-you-for-coming-to-my-headtalk.hotglue.me/

For those who prefer a formal introduction:

Ana(na)t (maria) Mart.kovich (AKA Mrs. So Bitch); a researcher of material culture, illustrator, curator and multi-disciplinary artist.Working in text, printmaking, moving image, assemblage-installation, dressing up and show-and-tell; Working through critical thinking, humor and melancholy; undertaking an existentially material exploration of everyday performative reality. Holding a BA in Fashion Design from Shenkar College (2011), an MA in cultural studies from the Hebrew University in Jerusalem (2019), and an AdMA from Sint Lucas Antwerp (2022).

*I would like to thank nadine for lending me a helping hand in printing, and a.pass for lending me a helping screen, Tamar Levit and Yaen Levi, and all my fellow scavengers, for their sharing and caring.

** during the 'open studio' there will some previous prints/publication for sale with the money going directly towards Gaza, Sudan and Congo fundraisers.



See you soon!!
