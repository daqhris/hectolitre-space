Open Studios at Hectolitre

by Melanie Ganino, Chris-Armel Iradukunda, Javi Fernández, Gregor Petrikovič, Maria Stuut & Jere

Open door, shower, marble, play. The machine started dreaming. It asked the empty space what it needed: an echo answered—‘100 litres of semifictional sand, to fill up the pool.’ We became allies: we the tiles and polaroids, we the voices and asteroids, AI archivists, semi-fictional characters, we the echo can encode the surrounding ruins. I close my eyes and imagine this room full of other-than-human companions—whoever crosses the boundary of our humanity playfully hugs non-humans next to us.

Thursday, Septembre 4th, 2025(12am-10pm)
Friday & Saturday, 5th & 6th September (6pm-9pm)
@Reactor, Rue de l'Hectolitre 3, Brussels

Accessibility: For accessibility inquiries, please contact us via direct message to arrange support.
