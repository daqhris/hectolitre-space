hook - an installation of a self-portrait by Madeline Zuzevich

Constructed in the aftermath of shift, hook oscillates between a fluid and static sense of self. The installation examines cycles of assembly and erasure through the self-portrait, a medium susceptible to tradition, yet liberating in its capacity to document transition.

Mediums: Film photography, sculpture, poetry.

Meetup: Thursday, Sep 29, 2022 at 20h

Madeline Zuzevich is Chicago-based writer and analog photographer. Her writing and research surrounds gender identity and sexuality, foregrounding the significance of spatial relations within the home.
She works across a variety of mediums, including language, photography, and sculpture. Her debut poetry chapbook, CAVITY, was released in June 2022.
