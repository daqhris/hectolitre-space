CURIEUCITY#3
in Marolles

Free three-day festival where science and art meet

Come to the Vossenplein area on 29 and 30 November and 1 December and be immersed in the 'mysteries of science and technology'. Discover how fascinating themes such as space travel, mathematics, mechanics, chemistry and physics can be. Together we will explore why certain things in science are considered true or false, and many more fascinating topics!

About the artist:

With One more drop, Ira Melkonyan continues her research on the performativity of liquids from a transnational feminist perspective, venturing into what is probably the most metaphorically charged bodily fluid. By centering blood and other biological substances as performative agents, this transdisciplinary performance explores the fantasy and mysticism surrounding fleeting bodies, political boundaries and a sense of connection.
