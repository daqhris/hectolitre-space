HIVING IN HECTOLITRE

by Ana Pinter

an afternoon session in Hectolitre’s garden



Monday, 25th of May, 2026
19:00-21:30h
Rue de l'Hectolitre 3, Brussels

This highly participatory event is inspired by the potential of diverse human and non-human members of natureculture environments. The open and enclosed spaces of the Hectolitre centre are organized through several interactive and multimedia stations in which visitors are invited to let go of their everyday activities and immerse themselves in intuitive, spontaneous, and carefree acts of imagination, collaboration, and cohabitation. As a way of resisting socially and politically organized relationships, let us dive into the leisure and pleasure of unintentionality and non-productive behavior. Let us simply be, hiving in Hecto!
The event is created and organized by Ana Pinter, stage director, educator and researcher, a member of Tri groša/Threepenny collective based in Belgrade, Serbia, with the immeasurable support by Hecto community and Culture Moves Europe.

Supported by Culture Moves Europe.
