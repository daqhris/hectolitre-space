Performing With Words – Artistic Vocal Workshop with Naama Freedman

-
In this workshop we will learn how to make the best use of our voices and gain confidence in front of other people. Whether singing a song or reading a poem, we will learn that it is not about having a “pretty voice”, but rather to mean the words we say. Through collective and individual work we will explore together new possibilities for expression.

-
Naama Freedman is an artist, singer, and musician with an experience of 20 years of performing on stages around the world – from cabaret shows to art performances, poetry readings and musicals.
www.naamafreedman.com

-
21.12 Wednesday 18:00-21:00
Participation: 10 Euro
Limited to 10 participants
