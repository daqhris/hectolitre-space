by Melanie Ganino, Deborah Ruffato, Javi Fernández, Gregor Petrikovič, Mrs. Kovich, Jere

an afternoon of art selling

Come to discover Hectolitre’s 2025 artists and step into their research and practice for an afternoon of art selling. Under meteor showers and with vibrating bodies, we are waking up undead matter and heading for the intensity of the solstice. Days are still expanding, and so are we!

Sunday June 08, 2025(11pm-18pm)
@Reactor, Rue de l'Hectolitre 3, Brussels

Melanie Ganino @melanie.ganino weaves her practice around photography, worldbuilding and witchcrafting into magical objects and daily allies for transformation.

∿
Deborah Ruffato deborahruffato.squarespace.com: Filmmaker and researcher, she explores figures, practices and knowledge emerging from an ecology of darkness, questioning cinema as a ritual to give form to immateriality.

Javi Fernández @javi.fesa / https://loquehacejavi.com/
∿
Gregor Petrikovič @gregor_petrikovic photographer-filmmaker exploring human connection and alienation in the digital age through a blend of philosophy, movement, and visual storytelling.
