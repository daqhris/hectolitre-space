✨ SLUTRELEASE ✨

by Slutcore, Consensual Soundsystem, and Jezebel Studio

a celebration of embodiment, sensuality, self- love and expression and the art of (erotic) dance

With Slutcore, Consensual Soundsystem, and Jezebel Studio, we’re creating a conscious club experience at Réacteur, honouring its history as a former swing club. Expect high-energy music, electrifying (pole) performances on the dance floor, and sexy activities and installations throughout the space—including a (former) shower and garden. This is a celebration of embodiment, sensuality, self- love and expression and the art of (erotic) dance.

☆ Yes, we are all sluts ☆

We’re here to reclaim the word “slut” and liberate it from its negative connotations and shame. We view the erotic as power and believe exploring sensuality is deeply healing and liberating. Come as your most sexy self—whether that’s a full kinky harness, a colourful feather costume, or a simple shirt and jeans. A dressing room with makeup and outfits will be available for you to explore your sexy alter ego.

☆ The Slutcore Collective Values ☆

This event is co-hosted by Slutcore, a collective of sluts for hardcore music, in collaboration with Consensual Soundsystem and Jezebel Studio. Their values are at the heart of this experience:

*Respect and Inclusivity*

When entering the space this shows you agree to our values. We celebrate diversity and ask for pronouns to ensure everyone feels seen and respected. This is a judgment-free zone where you can be your authentic self. Unity in diversity is key, and we believe in bringing people from all backgrounds and identities together to share in the joy of rave music.

*Safety and Care*

Your well-being is our priority. Our events include self-care and safety areas where you can recharge, relax, and talk to someone if you're feeling unwell or uncomfortable. We care for each other, which means looking out for one another to ensure everyone feels safe and respected.

*Boundaries and Consent*

Respect is non-negotiable, and consent is crucial in all interactions, whether on the dance floor or elsewhere. Everyone’s boundaries matter, and we ensure our space remains safe, supportive, and respectful.

*Community and Connection*

Slutcore is more than just a party collective; it’s a community of like-minded individuals who share a passion for rave music and celebrating life. Our events foster connections, friendships, and love, creating a space where everyone can belong.
Join us for a day of clubbing. Let’s reclaim the word “slut,” honour our sensuality, and create a space where everyone can fully express themselves without judgment. See you on the dance floor!
