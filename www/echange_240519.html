Lip-Sync Challenge

is an association founded in April 2021 with the aim of promoting the art of lip-syncing


We are an inclusive collective, sensitive to the values carried by the queer community. We create performances, shows and content based around playback. Through our work, we aspire to support artists, to showcase representations of multiple genders and non-genders, to include all kinds of individualities, to spread emotions and ideas. The association is based in Clermont-Ferrand (63000) and has around ten members. Gathered around a common charter, the members of the association are experimenting with a horizontal approach to the association's decision-making and artistic creation.)
