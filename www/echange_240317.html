SADISTIC SWINGCLUB

by SADISTIC MASH-UP


A trans-intestinal adventure on loop at @hectolitre.space. Get ready to be submerged in the slimey world of Shank Shnale and Tarandtula, accompanied by off-clerical organ sounds. Get loose on snotshots and witness the radical last cigarette immortalised in a coughpainting show.
