HECTOLITRE MEMBERS

Artists, cultural professionals and activists currently in residency at Hectolitre
Artistes, gestionnaire culturel et militants actuellement en résidence à Hectolitre

Mladen Bundalo
Born 1986 in Prijedor, Bosnia-Herzegovina.
An interdisciplinary artist working on visual-dialogical systems that address movement, exchange, value, inflation, and uncertainty, as nodal categories in the experience of modern society. Lives and works in Brussels.

mladenbundalo.com

Martina Petrović
Interdisciplinary artist, born in Serbia. Her artistic practice started with visual works, from drawing and painting to sculpture and installation. Currently she is busy with artistic research exploring the sphere of performative rituals. Her practice revolves around reroting of Balkan rituals into the sphere of environmental and paricipatory art. She is also active in two art collectives School of love and Garden of Delights.

cargocollective.com/TheLastStraw

Pierre-Louis Cassou
Film and audio-visual art producer (France, 1982).
Founder of La Tangente, production structure. Lives and works in Brussels, Belgium.

tangente.tv

Maria Stuut
Maria is a biologist turned artist with an inexplicable fondness for cabbage, working with film, performance, music, and writing. With a microscope in one hand and a mirror in the other, she lets her body wander into encounters, exploring her environment through a personal, lyrical and tragicomic lens.

mubi.com/nl/cast/maria-stuut

Gregor Petrikovič
Gregor Petrikovič is a Slovak-British artist working with film and movement. His work explores human connection and alienation in the era of digital disconnect, often drawing inspiration from his background in philosophy, photography, and movement. He completed an MA in Photography at the Royal College of Art as a Burberry Design Scholar.
His films have been exhibited internationally, including International Documentary Film Festival Amsterdam, Sónar Barcelona, Tate Late, the Athens Digital Arts Festival, and The Swiss Archive of the Performing Arts. He is developing his ongoing film Sincerely, Victor Pike through Film London’s FLAMIN Fellowship whilst being a resident at Hectolitre.

gregorpetrikovic.com/film
instagram.com/gregor_petrikovic

Chris-Armel Iradukunda
Burundian, undocumented, artist born in 1992. For eight years, he documented walks in Belgium with an art form that caused his exile: photography. At Hectolitre, he furthers research for independent and performance projects. In his toolbox are stored computer engineering skills, fluency in Eurasian languages, on-screen roles in Chinese and Dutch films, etc.

awalkaday.art
instagram.com/walk.day
daqhris.com
daqhris.com/venice-biennale

Jeremias Pérez Salerno
Jere was born in 1990 in Argentina. He is a musician, songwriter and poet. Self perceived as an intuition wanderer, he passed his twenties travelling with his music in Europe and South America, till finding in Brussels the perfect environment to finally settle down. He is author of several poetry fanzines and co-author of many songs from the Argentinian indie rock project Montarosa.

instagram.com/hola.jere.ps

Mélanie Ganino
Melanie Ganino’s worldbuilding practice weaves photography performance and community arts to create bridges between knowledge, disciplines and forms of life, imagining other ways of sensing, relating to otherness and inhabiting our environments.

instagram.com/melanie.ganino

Max Lurye

Davide Paolillo
Davide Paolillo (1992) is a Brussels-based Italian composer, sound artist and PhD researcher. His practice is grounded in direct experimentation with instrument deformations, noisy machines and creative coding.
Davide has collaborated with international musicians, ensembles, and institutions and his music has been performed at international festivals. He teaches Music Theory in the Drama department of the Royal Conservatory of Antwerp, as well as being founding member and electronic performer of the Rhizome Ensemble.

ap-arts.be/en/person/davide-paolillo
instagram.com/_davidepaolillo

Carlo Sampaolesi
Carlo Sampaolesi (b. 1997, Italy) is an accordionist and performer based in Brussels, and a resident artist at Hectolitre.
His research into the instrument's timbral and spectral range has led to commissions and collaborations with composers including Francesca Verunelli, Dmitri Kourliandski, Gavin Bryars, Giulia Lorusso, Maurizio Azzan, Carlo Elia Praderio and Mattia Clera, and performances with ensembles including the Neue Vocalsolisten, mdi ensemble, FontanaMIX Ensemble, Opificio Sonoro, the Orchestra del Teatro La Fenice, Eutopia Ensemble and Dedalus Ensemble.
He is also organiser and artistic director of the PNE.UMA Contemporary Accordion Festival.

carlosampaolesi.com
carlosampaolesi.bandcamp.com
instagram.com/sampaolesic

Ychaï Gassenbauer
Ychaï Gassenbauer is a Brussels-based audiovisual artist working across animation, video, and performance. Originally from Jerusalem, his multidisciplinary practice explores identity, intimacy, and belief, while questioning the cultural structures that shape them.

ychaigassenbauer.com
vimeo.com/ychaigassenbauer

Ana Edwards
Ana Edwards is a Chilean artist and filmmaker. Her work combines aspects of ethnography, ecology, poetry with the perception to explore relationships and systems between beings and their environment. She studied Visual Arts in Santiago, and then an MA in Visual Anthropology in the UK. She currently lives between Belgium and France where she recently completed a program at Le Fresnoy Studio National des Arts Contemporains.

ethnofest.gr/directors/ana-edwards
