On Saturday, January 6th, we will have the opportunity to experience L=10, a minor tragedy about an inability to L=10.
To overcome this incompetence a loudspeaker-headed-puppet prepares to leave I behind in order to enter the world of U.

Project has received support from Sound Development City, Frame Contemporary Art Finland, and Arts Promotion Centre Finland and it has been presented in Poland, Germany, Serbia, Kosovo, Finland, North-Macedonia, Belgium, Greece and Slovenia.

Ari-Pekka Leinonen works with performatic sound art installations that utilise sound projection with multiple loudspeakers. Other than that he is interested in carpenting, possibilities of loudspeakers as wooden sculptures and how the sound systems create platforms for moving bodies and for sonic storytelling. During 2023, he was exploring the skyline of Brussels and minor details of the city facade in order to combine the visual explorations and aural discoveries into a conclusive soundpiece ele-gi-eli for his sound triptych consisting of two earlier works L=10 and ||: o / dear<3 :||
