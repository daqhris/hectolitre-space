30 September at 19h

A LACK OF WEIGHT & GRAVITY

Screening and artist talk: Nada Arnaut & Igor Bošnjak
Curated by Mladen Bundalo


A lack of weight & gravity is a concept which collects several ideas and works around the idea of flying, hoovering, floating, etc. Mostly these works are questioning camera as a moving and flying eye or subject alone as an object who resist to gravity.
Videos are compilation of Bosnia & Herzegovina artists Nada Aranut & Igor Bosnjak who works in several contemporary mediums.

List of videos for screening:
A monument to the fallen war heroes of a future war / Igor Bosnjak / 2017 / 3 min 47 sec Floating in the most peculiar way / Nada Arnaut / 2012 / 3 min 45 sec
Is there death after life / Igor Bosnjak / 2018 / 7 min 30 sec
Yugoslavian space programme / Nada Arnaut & Igor Bosnjak / 2012 / 6 min 06 sec
Mecha / Igor Bosnjak / 2018 / 5 min 40 sec
Total duration: around 27 minutes

Along the screening and talk, artists and curator will prepare you the best “uštipci”, traditional Balkans pancakes.
